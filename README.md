Becoming a Data Scientist involves combining many different fields like Computer Science and Mathematics. Data Scientists identify trends in data, build data pipelines that move information, and communicate their findings to stakeholders, making them essential to modern decision-making. This work presents the best practices for becoming a Data Scientist, starting from college.

# Activities to Develop Skills
This section discusses activities that increase your proficiency and overall credibility to others in the field.

## LeetCode
Practice LeetCode problems to prepare for technical interviews. This sharpens your problem-solving skills and improves confidence in your ability. 




## Kaggle
Kaggle is more specialized in Data Science than LeetCode.




## Personal Projects
Creating personal projects are an important part of your portfolio because they show the application of your technical knowledge into a product. Start with an idea that is familiar or adjacent to something you have done before in a class like mapping out the weather.

# Networking and Practical Experience
This section discusses activities that increase your contacts.

## Competing in Hackathons
Take part in these events to develop collaboration skills as you must work in a team to build a product under time pressure, similar to a real-world environment. This helps you meet people in the industry like employers and mentors who you can connect to on platforms like LinkedIn. Follow-up with those people after the hackathon ends to better stand out to them.

Go to UWB Hacks as one your first hackathons as it is suited for people of all skill levels including beginners like you. Show up to other events aside from the hackathon like the workshops. It is best to form a team by March as this event happens around April.

## Joining Clubs
Forming connection with peers who have the same interests as you by going to clubs is significant in widening your network and immersing you in a practical setting that is hands-on. Attend any club meetings from the Association for Computing Machinery by monitoring their Discord for any news of the first one.

Another club you can apply to the UW Data Science Society in the Seattle campus as soon the form opens Winter Quarter, which will further connect you to more hackathons.
