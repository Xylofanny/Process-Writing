The groundwork to becoming a Data Scientist involves combining many different fields like Computer Science and Mathematics.
Tasks assigned to Data Scientists include identifying trends in data, using data pipelines to move information, and communicating findings to higher-ups [1].
This work presents the best practices on how to become a Data Scientist starting from college.


# Activities to Develop Experience
This section discusses activities that increase your proficiency and overall credibility to others in the field.

## Personal Projects
Creating personal projects are an important part of your portfolio because they show the application of your technical knowledge into a product. Start with an idea that is familiar or adjacent to something you have done before in a class like mapping out the weather.

## Hackathons
You should take part in these events to develop collaboration skills as you must work in a team to build a product under time pressure, similar to a real-world environment. This helps you meet people in the industry like employers and mentors who you can connect to on platforms like LinkedIn. Follow-up with those people after the hackathon ends to better stand out to them.

Go to UWB Hacks as one your first hackathons as it is suited for people of all skill levels including beginners like you [2]. Show up to other events aside from the hackathon like the workshops. It is best to form a team by March as this event happens around April.

## LeetCode
Practice LeetCode problems to prepare for technical interviews. This sharpens your problem-solving skills and improves confidence in your ability. 

## Clubs
Forming connection with peers who have the same interests as you by going to clubs is significant in widening your network and immersing you in a practical setting that is hands-on. Attend any club meetings from the Association for Computing Machinery by monitoring their Discord for any news of the first one.

Another club you can apply to the UW Data Science Society in the Seattle campus as soon the form opens Winter Quarter, which will further connect you to more hackathons.
